History of Ballarat Tech School

Ballarat is a regional city in western Victoria, Australia, best known for its role in the 1850s gold rush. The discovery of gold transformed Ballarat into one of Australia's wealthiest inland cities.

One of Ballarat's most significant historical events was the Eureka Rebellion, where miners protested against mining licences and government authority. This event became an important symbol of Australian democracy.

Today Ballarat combines its historic heritage with modern education, tourism and innovation. Ballarat Tech School continues this tradition by inspiring students to explore emerging technologies and future careers.
