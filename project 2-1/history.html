History of Ballarat Tech School

Learn more about our background and story

Ballarat is a regional city in western Victoria, Australia, best known for its role in the 1850s gold rush. The discovery of gold in 1851 transformed Ballarat from a small settlement into one of Australia’s wealthiest inland cities during the Victorian gold rush era. One of the most significant historical events associated with Ballarat was the Eureka Rebellion, where gold miners protested against mining licences and government authority. This event later became an important symbol of democracy and workers’ rights in Australia. Today, Ballarat combines its historic heritage with modern education, tourism, and culture. Major attractions include Sovereign Hill, a living museum recreating gold rush life, as well as art galleries, historic architecture, and cultural festivals. Ballarat also developed into an important education hub. Institutions such as Federation University Australia originated from the historic Ballarat School of Mines, founded in 1870
