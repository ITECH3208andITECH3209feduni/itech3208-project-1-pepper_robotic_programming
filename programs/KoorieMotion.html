About the Program

  • This two day experience brings together Koorie students from Years 6 and 7 for hands on activities, cultural learning, and plenty of fun.
  • the program focuses on marngrook, the traditional Indigenous ball game that helped inspire modern Aussie Rules. Over the two days, students will take part in practical workshops.
  • this includes studentsmaking their own leather wallets, along with outdoor activities inspired by marngrook.
