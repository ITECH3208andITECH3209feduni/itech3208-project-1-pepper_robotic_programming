Robot Missions

About the Program

  • In this one day program, students work in pairs to assemble their VEX IQ robot and explore sensors to replicate a known real world example (e.g. robotic vacuum).
  • Construct a basic VEX IQ robot.
  • Add and code attachments including lights and sensors.
  • Experiment with different technologies and software.
  • Engage in basic drone flying and coding.
  • Design and execute a search & retrieval mission.
