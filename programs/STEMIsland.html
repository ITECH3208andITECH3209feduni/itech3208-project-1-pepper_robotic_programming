About the Program

  • Students arrive at the Tech School, only to hear about a baffling mystery that has the local community stumped. Although the scenario is fictitious, students will need to learn and apply real-life skills in cybersecurity, Virtual Reality (VR) and forensics to help them solve this "whodunit" mystery.
  • Improve their knowledge of cyber security.
  • Be exposed to applications of VR in the real world.
  • Develop an understanding of how forensics uses scientific reasoning.
